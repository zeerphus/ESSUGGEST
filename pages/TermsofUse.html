1.Acceptance of These Terms

By creating an account or using ESSUGGEST, you agree to these Terms of Use together with the Community Guidelines, the Data Privacy Policy, the Security Policy, and the IP Rights Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2.Eligibility and Accounts

  • ESSUGGEST is intended for students of Eastern Samar State University.
  • You must verify your email address before your first login, and verify your ESSU credentials (student number, course, section, year, and college) before you can post.
  • The information you provide must be accurate and your own.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. Keep your login credentials private, as set out in the Security Policy.

3.Your Content

You keep authorship of the suggestions, polls, and comments you submit. By submitting content, you grant the University a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to review, discuss, and, if selected, implement it as part of university operations. Do not submit content that is not yours to share. See the IP Rights Policy for details.

4.Acceptable Use

You agree to follow the Community Guidelines when you post, comment, vote, or upvote. In particular, you agree not to harass or threaten others, post spam, disclose another person's private information, knowingly present false claims as fact, or post unlawful or sexually explicit content.

5.Anonymous Posting

Posts are anonymous to other students by default. Anonymity does not apply to the system itself: your account remains internally linked to your post so that moderators can act on violations. Anonymous posts are held to the same standard as named posts, and misuse may lead to the loss of this option.

6.Moderation and Enforcement

Reported content and suspicious activity are reviewed by the moderation team. Depending on the severity of a violation, consequences may range from content removal and a formal warning, to temporary restrictions on posting, to suspension of your account. Reports follow the Reporting Handbook. If you believe your content was removed in error, you may appeal by emailing essuggest@gmail.com.

7.No Guarantee of Action

Posting, upvoting, or voting does not oblige the University to adopt, respond to, or implement any suggestion or poll result.

8.Availability and Changes to the Service

ESSUGGEST may be updated, restricted, or temporarily unavailable, and features may change. No online system can guarantee uninterrupted service or absolute security.

9.Changes to These Terms

These terms may be revised from time to time. Continued use of ESSUGGEST after a revision means you accept the updated terms.

10.Contact

Questions about these terms may be sent to essuggest@gmail.com.

Questions about the terms of use?
