1.Purpose of Reporting

ESSUGGEST depends on the active participation of students in identifying content that violates the Community Guidelines. As moderators cannot review every post, a timely report from a user is often the most effective means of ensuring that inappropriate content is addressed promptly.

2.Content Eligible for Reporting

  • Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
  • Hate speech or identity-based attacks.
  • Spam or unsolicited promotional content.
  • Unauthorized disclosure of another individual's private information.
  • Misinformation presented as verified fact.
  • Any other content that violates the Community Guidelines.

3.Procedure for Reporting a Post or Comment

Every suggestion, poll, and comment includes an associated reporting function, accessed as follows:

  1. Open the post or comment intended for reporting.
  2. Select the report or flag option and choose the reason that most closely applies.
  3. Provide a brief explanatory note with any context that may assist the moderation team.
  4. Submit the report. A confirmation of receipt will be provided.

4.Review Process

All reports are reviewed by the moderation team. Reported content is not automatically removed while under review, as not every report results in a confirmed violation; this practice is intended to preserve fairness toward the original author.

Reporters are not always notified of the outcome of their report, in accordance with privacy considerations. Where content is ultimately removed, however, its author is informed of the specific guideline that was violated.

5.Appeals

A student whose content has been removed and who believes the decision was made in error may respond to the notification received, or submit an appeal by email to essuggest@gmail.com, explaining the grounds on which reconsideration is requested.

6.Urgent or Safety-Related Concerns

Where a post presents an immediate safety concern rather than a routine violation of community standards, students should not rely on the standard in-app reporting process. Instead, they are advised to contact essuggest@gmail.com directly, or to notify their campus's Human Resources Management Office without delay.

Questions about the reporting handbook?

Write to us at essuggest@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
