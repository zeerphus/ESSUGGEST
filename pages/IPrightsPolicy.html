1.Ownership of Submissions

Users retain authorship of the ideas and content they submit. Posting on ESSUGGEST does not transfer ownership of a suggestion to Eastern Samar State University or to ESSUGGEST. By submitting content, however, the user grants the University a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to review, discuss, and, if selected, implement the suggestion as part of university operations.

2.Ownership of the Platform

The ESSUGGEST name, logo, and interface design are the property of the project and Eastern Samar State University. These elements may not be copied, redistributed, or repurposed for other projects without prior authorization.

3.Originality of Submissions

Users must not submit content that is not their own to share. This includes, without limitation, reproducing another individual's written work without attribution, or submitting a suggestion authored by a third party who has not consented to its posting.

4.Comments and Discussion

The terms set forth in this policy apply equally to comments and discussion replies as to original suggestions and polls. Authorship is retained by the user, and the same non-exclusive license applies.

5.Disputes and Inquiries

A user who believes that their intellectual property rights have been affected by content on ESSUGGEST may raise the matter by contacting essuggest@gmail.com.

Questions about this policy?

Write to us at essuggest@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
