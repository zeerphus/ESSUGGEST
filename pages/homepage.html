Polls and suggestions

Vote on it, or suggest it

ESSUGGEST gives every student two ways to shape ESSU — raise something new, or weigh in on what's already on the table.

POLLS

Students can post polls. Cast your vote, watch results update live, and see exactly where the campus stands.

  • One vote per student
  • 24-hour voting window
  • Results stay visible

SUGGESTIONS

Post an idea, tag it, and choose whether your name shows. Other students upvote and comment — the ones with the most support rise to the top.

  • Upvoting
  • Threaded discussion
  • Optional anonymity

How it works

Three steps from idea to answers

  1. 01

    Verify your ESSUGGEST account

    Sign up with your ESSU credentials — student number, course, year, section, and college — to verify you're a student before you can post, comment, vote, or upvote.

  2. 02

    Post a suggestion or vote in a poll

    Write up your idea and choose whether your name shows, or cast your vote on an open poll — one vote per student, open for 24 hours.

  3. 03

    Watch it rise

    Other students upvote and reply; the most-supported ideas rise to the top, and poll results stay visible to everyone after voting closes.
