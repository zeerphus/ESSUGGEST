Two ways to be heard

Vote on it, or Suggest it

ESSUGGEST gives every student two ways to shape ESSU — raise something new, or weigh in on what's already on the table.

POLLS

Students can post polls. Cast your vote, watch results update live, and see exactly where the campus stands.

Four steps from idea to answers

01

Verify your ESSUGGEST account

Sign up with your ESSU credentials — student number, course, year, section, and college — to verify you're a student before you can post, comment, vote, or upvote.

02

Post a suggestion, choose anonymous or named

Write up your idea and choose whether your name shows. You can also cast your vote on an open poll.

03

Others upvote or reply; the top ones rise

Suggestions get upvoted and discussed; polls fill up with votes in real time.

04

See where things stand

Suggestions rise or fall with upvotes and discussion. Poll results get published for everyone to see.
