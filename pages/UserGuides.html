1.About ESSUGGEST

ESSUGGEST is the official online suggestion platform of Eastern Samar State University. It supersedes the traditional paper suggestion box, providing a platform through which suggestions may be submitted, reviewed, and upvoted, and through which the University may issue formal responses. Participation may take the form of posting or voting on suggestions, or casting votes in official polls.

2.Creating an Account

Prospective users must register using their email address. Upon registration, a verification link is sent to the address provided; this link must be activated prior to the user's first login.

  1. Access the Create Account page and complete the required fields.
  2. Check the registered email inbox for the verification message.
  3. Verify the email address, then log in using ESSUGGEST created account credentials.
  4. Users may need to submit their ESSU credentials (e.g. Student number, Course, Section, Year, College) in their profile menu for verification of account and to be able to post.

3.Submitting a Suggestion

Once logged in, students may submit suggestions for the University's consideration. Each suggestion may be assigned a category to facilitate discoverability.

  1. Compose the suggestion clearly and in a constructive manner.
  2. Select an appropriate tag or category.
  3. Posts are anonymous by default; toggle this setting off in the composer if the post should appear under the user's name instead.
  4. Submit the suggestion; upon submission, it becomes visible to the student body.

4.Anonymity Policy

ESSUGGEST employs a hybrid anonymity model. Posts are anonymous by default, and users may toggle this setting off, on a per-post basis, to have their identity disclosed instead. Anonymous posts remain subject to the same moderation standards and Community Guidelines applicable to posts submitted under one's name.

5.Upvoting and Commenting

Students may express support for a suggestion by upvoting it, and may participate in threaded discussion through comments posted beneath it. Suggestions that receive greater support tend to be ranked more prominently within the feed.

6.Participating in Polls

Polls are questions posed directly to the student body by the University. Each poll permits one vote per student and remains open for a twenty-four-hour period. Results are updated in real time as votes are submitted, and the poll remains visible after its closure so that all users may view the final outcome.

7.Requesting Assistance

Should any technical difficulty arise, or should a question remain unanswered by this guide, students are encouraged to consult the FAQ page or contact essuggest@gmail.com.
