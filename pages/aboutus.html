About ESSUGGEST

A suggestion box that actually moves!

ESSUGGEST is Eastern Samar State University's online suggestion and polling platform — built so student feedback stops disappearing into a locked box and starts moving: posted, voted on, and discussed in the open.

Why we built this

The paper suggestion box works one way. A student drops in a note, and from that point on, no one else sees it, no one can back it up, and there's rarely a way to know if anything happened next.

ESSUGGEST makes the process visible. Suggestions can be upvoted and discussed by other students, and polls show live results instead of a single silent tally — so feedback stays out in the open instead of disappearing into a box.
