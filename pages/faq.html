Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out at essuggest@gmail.com.

How do I create an account? ▾

Go to Create Account or Get started and sign up using your email address. You'll get a verification link by email — click it, then log in with your ESSUGGEST credentials.

Do I need to verify anything before I can post, vote, or comment? ▾

Yes. Verifying your email gets you logged in, but posting, commenting, voting, and upvoting all require one more step: submitting your ESSU credentials — student number, course, section, year, and college — through your profile. This confirms you're an actual ESSUdyante before you can interact with anything on the platform.

Is anonymous posting really anonymous? ▾

Posts are anonymous by default — your name isn't shown to other students unless you choose to turn that off for a given post. Anonymous posts still follow the same Community Guidelines as named ones, so the option can be revoked if it's repeatedly abused.

How does voting on a poll work? ▾

Each poll allows one vote per student and stays open for a 24-hour voting window. Results update live as votes come in, and the poll stays visible after voting closes so everyone can see the final outcome.

How do I report inappropriate content? ▾

Every suggestion, poll, and comment has a report option attached to it. Open the post, select the report/flag option, choose a reason, and submit — the moderation team reviews it from there. See the Reporting Handbook for the full walkthrough.

Will I know if ESSU responds to my suggestion? ▾

ESSUGGEST doesn't currently have a built-in status-tracking feature for individual suggestions. Visibility mostly comes through upvotes and comments from other students — any official response or action from ESSU would be communicated separately rather than shown as a status label on the post itself.

Can I edit or delete a suggestion after posting it? ▾

You can delete your own posts at any time from your account. If you need to change something after other students have already started commenting or upvoting, deleting and reposting is usually clearer than editing.
