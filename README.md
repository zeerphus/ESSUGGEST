ESSUGGEST is a website application where students and teachers can share their ideas to make the school even better. If you notice something that needs fixing, have a cool idea for an event, or want to give a suggestion, you can easily type it in and send it to the suggestion feed. You can even choose to send your message without sharing your name! It gives everyone an easy way to speak up and help make the university a super happy, fun, and safe place for all.
